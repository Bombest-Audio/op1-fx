## Hold time per fragment

A fragment is PHONIC cycles of the input, played BAUD times at the TONE rate before the core moves to a newer one, so the hold is BAUD x PHONIC / (f0 x rate). This plugin has no time control, so nothing can sync to tempo; the numbers below are only valid for the stated input pitch. At 90 BPM: quarter 667 ms, 1/8 333 ms, 1/16 167 ms.

Ad-lib Throw is the one tuned to a grid: BAUD 13 gives about 650 ms, a quarter note at 90 BPM, for a 200 Hz voice. Any other pitch drifts off the grid.

## Honest limits

- Phone is a cycle-fragment repitching glitcher, not a band-pass EQ. Devvon's telephone recipe (boost 2 to 4k, about +10 dB near 1.8 kHz, strip lows and highs, big reverb after) and his 657 Hz to 2.6 kHz and 500 Hz to 3k fidelity ranges cannot be reproduced here. Ad-lib Line and Lo-Fi Moment only approximate the thin, chattery character; use an EQ before or after.
- The wet loop is gated by the input level (about 10 ms follower), so it cannot ring after the phrase ends. A real throw needs a reverb or delay after it.
- Input with a fundamental below 60 Hz never completes a fragment (the longest fragment is PHONIC / 60 s), so a sine 808 or kick below that is silent in the wet path unless it has harmonics. Kick Knock and 808 Translate assume the click, upper sweep, or a fundamental of 60 Hz or more.
- Fragments are at least 128 samples (2.9 ms at 44.1 kHz), so noise and high content behave as if PHONIC were larger.
- Devvon has no direct guidance on repitch-looper effects, or on kick, snare, 808, drum bus, pad or FX use of this one. Those rows say `none: derived from DSP`. The shared philosophy applied is a low mix on most presets ("a little bit goes such a long way", NA__XtbSdseGHxI.md), keeping the lead clear, and automating the lo-fi for moments (NA__86sFuxuydeI.md).
- Checked by running each preset through the core on a synthetic signal (200 Hz voice-like, noise, 65 Hz saturated sine, chord): all produce finite output with an audible wet component. Not auditioned by ear.
