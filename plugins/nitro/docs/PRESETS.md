# Nitro factory presets

12 presets in `mpc/presets.json`, shared by the MPC VST2 port and the JUCE plugin. Preset 0 is Init and equals the OP-1 factory defaults (FREQ 0.01526, RES 0, SHAPE 0.46609, DRIVE 0.48831, MIX 1). Every preset sets MIX to 1 (fully wet) so recalling one gives a known sound whatever MIX was before. Values in the JSON are normalized 0..1; this table shows the real-world targets they were computed from. The converter is `nitro_convert.py` (session scratchpad); it implements the exact inverse of `core/nitro_core.c` and forward-checks every value (cutoff within 0.005 percent, shape rows exact, drive within 0.001 dB, loop gain within 0.001).

Nitro now has a MIX knob (added after these presets were designed, all set to 1), so parallel blends can also be done with MIX instead of a send. "Parallel" below was written for a send or parallel bus, and nothing here is tempo-synced. It also filters L and R identically, so Devvon's mid/side "clean and wide" 808 trick (NA__S-4rqmuXuYU.md) has no equivalent and was not attempted. DRIVE has no output make-up, so drive presets come out louder by roughly the drive amount until the soft ceiling bites; trim after the plugin.

## Conversion notes

- FREQ: `hz = 14 * (16000/14)^n`, so `n = ln(hz/14) / ln(16000/14)`.
- SHAPE: row position `n * 11`, so row `k` sits at `k/11`. Rows are LP 24, LP 18, LP 12, LP 6, THRU, HP 6+, NOTCH, BAND, HP 6, HP 12, HP 18, HP 24. A fractional position crossfades the two neighbouring mix rows linearly (the mix coefficients, not the frequency responses). Resonant Wail sits at 6.5, halfway between NOTCH and BAND. Init sits at 5.127, between HP 6+ and NOTCH.
- DRIVE: gain is `10^(1.2 d^2)`, which is `24 d^2` dB, so `d = sqrt(dB / 24)`. 0 to +24 dB.
- RES: the naive inverse is wrong because feedback depends on DRIVE and on the signal. Loop gain is `4.4 * clamp(res^2 * (0.8 + 0.2 d) + 0.2 * res * env, 0, 1)`. Targets are the loop gain at silence (env 0), solved after DRIVE is fixed. The second column of the loop-gain figure is the value at env 1. The loop rings on its own at 4.0, so only Resonant Wail ever crosses it, and only with signal.
- No parameter in Nitro is an int, so there are no cell or raw-unit conversions. The `act` key is never written.
- Sanity check run against the real C core: swept sines through every preset gave the expected passbands and cut slopes, output stayed finite, and no preset rang after a noise burst.

## Devvon coverage

Direct guidance exists for the ad-lib telephone EQ (NA__9LYLrDEWcBM.md), the 808 heard band of 350 Hz to 2 kHz and the 6.19 dB boost on the 571 Hz to 8.2 kHz band (NA__B7iw3WNpPA4.md, NA__MRXKMKC-_Vg.md), the sub boost at 30 Hz (NA__S-4rqmuXuYU.md), drum bus saturation for crack (NA__3gsTpFBCJ2w.md), and the 6.19 dB boost figure (counted above). The vocal drive preset takes only the philosophy of driving a vocal early in the chain for harmonics and presence from NA__NaVHB-7N0wQ.md (his "7" is a knob position on another plugin and is not used), and Keys Telephony takes only the idea of a low cut that thins an instrument toward a telephone tone from NA__Y0_G0R9tHoo.md (his example is a guitar with no end frequency); their numbers are derived from DSP. The throw, snare, kick, pad and muffled-drop settings are my own reading of his philosophy applied to this DSP. NA__NywJxCLsEgg.md is an interview where the muffled bridge is discussed as a filter move, with no numbers. Kick Thump and Resonant Wail have no Devvon basis.
