# Cwo faceplate brief

Canvas 1280 x 630 (the MPC plugin view size; the desktop window scales it at the same aspect ratio).

- Plate color `#07080c`, ink `#e9ece8`, muted `#6c7370`, track `#23262a`.
- Accents: blue `#698eff` (FREQ), violet `#a77bff` (DELAY), red `#ff4d5e` (FDBACK), mint `#2ef0a0` (SIDEBAND). These are the house
  palette, picked to read on the plate. I did not sample them from the OP-1 screen.
- Type: the faceplate design used Barlow Condensed. The plugin draws with the default JUCE sans squeezed to 82% width, so the labels are
  close to it but not the same face, and it has not been compared side by side.
- Center: a Holstein cow, generated with Higgsfield (`design/art/cow-c.png`, no halo, flat black backdrop), because the OP-1 screen for
  this effect is a cow. Scaled to 636 px at x 322, y -14, with a thin mint floor line under the hooves at y 528.
  Alternates kept in `design/art/`: `cow-a` and `cow-b` have a halo baked in, which can't be animated.
  The cow is an original render. It is not the OP-1 artwork and not a likeness of any real animal or person.
- Halo: 48 short ticks in violet, mint, red and blue, centre (506, 214), radius 150 to 190, drawn live behind the cow. It turns with the
  shifter (direction from SIDEBAND, speed from FREQ, capped at a third of a turn per second) and brightens with the level of the shifted
  echoes. The cow's eye gets a small cyan glow on the same level. Nothing else moves.
- Alpha: `faceplate-bg-1280x630.png` is opaque on the cow and the floor line and transparent elsewhere. Under the transparent pixels the RGB
  holds the plate color and a dim static halo, for hosts that ignore alpha. Rebuild with `python3 -I design/make_faceplate.py`.
- Left column: FREQ above DELAY. Right column: FDBACK above SIDEBAND. Each knob is a 240 x 250 cell: label (36 px), 270 degree ring
  (radius 74, stroke 6.2), readout (76 px), caption (12 px).
- MIX: a fifth, smaller knob (0.42 scale of the 240 x 250 cell) in the top right corner, centred over FDBACK, in cyan `#4fd8ff`
  with the caption DRY TO WET. The four main knobs and the halo are unchanged. On the MPC it is a knob of radius 34 at the same spot,
  with a row of 12 cyan ticks under it (`mpc/images/mix.png`, 33 frames) that fill from the left as MIX goes up.
- Wordmark CWO top left at (48, 24) with FREQUENCY SHIFT DELAY under it.

The layout was designed in Claude Design: https://claude.ai/design/p/96aff8c8-6222-4097-9f8b-19492ae4455b. The art and the live halo
are mine to wire in, so the plugin source (`plugin/PluginEditor.cpp`) is the source of truth for what is on screen.
Interactions: drag a knob vertically, 220 px for the full range, double-click to reset.

Files: `editor-preview.png` (rest), `anim-rest.png` (no signal) and `anim-signal.png` (halo at full level, quarter turn offset) are
renders from the CwoSnapshot tool.

## MPC animation

The MPC skin (`mpc/`) moves like the OP-1 screen for this effect (`bode.svg` in the firmware display folder). Everything is regenerated by
`python3 -I mpc/make_art.py`; it writes `mpc/images/bg.png` and five filmstrips. `bg.png` is MPC only (the cow with no static halo, plus dim
slots for the animated parts). `design/art/faceplate-bg-1280x630.png` is untouched.

What I recovered from `bode.svg`, by element id and colour:
- 13 vertical ticks that start stacked at the left end of the floor line and spread along it: the echo taps. I tied them to DELAY (spacing from
  4 percent to 100 percent of the line). The later 6 ticks are taller in the SVG, I kept that.
- 10 red bars in a column: I tied them to FDBACK, filling from the bottom (the last bar fades in, so it is smooth). The SVG does not say what
  the 10 steps mean, so the count-to-knob mapping is mine.
- A diamond of 6 stacked lines, upper 3 and lower 3, with the red copy `rr0..rr5` on top: the sideband icon. The upper half lights with the
  upper sideband, the lower half with the lower one. In the engine SIDEBAND 1.0 is upper only, 0.5 is ring mod and 0 is lower only, so I light
  the upper half at brightness `s` and the lower at `1 - s`.
- A track with end ticks, a centre tick and a blue dot plus blue line (`blue_1_`, `bluecenter`): I tied the dot to FREQ, travelling left to
  right with a blue line trailing it. The SVG only shows the resting pose, so the travel direction is my guess.

What I invented: the colours (house palette, not the OP-1 colours: blue FREQ, violet DELAY, red FDBACK, mint SIDEBAND), the halo arc, and all
positions. The halo arc is the level meter (`act`): 37 ticks on a half ellipse above the cow. Signal level brightens and lengthens them and adds
a faint bloom; the FREQ knob axis marches them along the arc by one colour cycle over the 8 knob steps so it wraps with no seam. The arc is a
half ellipse (700 x 190) because the strip limit of 12288 px divided by 64 frames leaves 192 px per frame.

I could not look at it on the device. The wrapper refreshes about every 100 ms, so the motion is gentle by design.
